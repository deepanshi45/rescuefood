About Food Rescue

Welcome to Food Rescue, a platform dedicated to reducing food waste and fighting hunger. We connect generous donors (like restaurants and individuals) with users like you who can rescue surplus food before it goes to waste.

Our Mission

Every year, millions of tons of food are wasted while many go hungry. Food Rescue aims to bridge this gap by making it easy to donate and claim edible food in your local area.

How It Works

  1. Donors Post: Admins or verified users list available food items with details like location and expiry.
  2. You Explore: Browse and search for items near you.
  3. Rescue & Collect: Claim an item and pick it up to save it from waste.

Impact So Far

We've helped rescue over 10,000 meals! Join us in making a difference.
